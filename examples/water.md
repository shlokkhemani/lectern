## Almost all of it is salty

::: chart water format=%
Oceans: 96.5
Ice: 1.7
Groundwater: 1.7
Rivers and lakes: 0.01
:::

Almost all of Earth's water [grow water] sits in the oceans. The fresh water we drink from [hl water:4] rivers
and lakes is a sliver too thin to see.
